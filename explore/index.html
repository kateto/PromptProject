What the shares mean

Share of conversations counts every conversation, so heavy users count for more. Share of people is the percentage of people in a group who had at least one conversation mainly about the subject. A dash marks a figure withheld because too few conversations, or a handful of people, stand behind it.

How subjects were coded

A language model read the person’s side of each conversation and named its main subject from a list of 28, or marked it as unclear or not on the list. Creative writing was added to the list later, so its share is a lower bound.

Who is in the data

65,689 adults on a US research panel, counting the conversations they had at 18 or older, July 2025 to June 2026. Age groups use each person’s age in July 2026. The panel is not a random sample of the country, so compare groups rather than reading any figure as national. More about the data.
