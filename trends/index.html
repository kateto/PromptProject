Our list of terms

The terms this site tracks, by broad category, most mentioned first. The list is a selection: a name or word that is not on it may well come up in the conversations; it simply has not been added here. Terms mentioned too rarely to chart month by month are left out.

How mentions are counted

A conversation mentions a term when the words appear in one of the person’s own messages, ignoring capital letters and punctuation; ChatGPT’s replies are not counted. Spelling variants and nicknames count, and some phrases are excluded on purpose (“hot dog” does not count as a dog). A mention says nothing about what the conversation is mainly about.

“Share of conversations” is how many of every 1,000 conversations mention the term. “Share of people” is the percentage of people who mentioned it at least once: over the year for the table and the groups, and among the people who used ChatGPT that month for the chart. Conversation shares give more weight to people who use ChatGPT heavily; people shares count everyone once.

Figures cover conversations by adults (18 and over), July 2025 to June 2026, in the panelist’s local time. A figure is published only when at least 20 different people stand behind it, and is withheld when five people supply more than half of it. A month with fewer mentions than that appears as a hollow point inside the hatched band, whose top is the publishing threshold: the true value is somewhere below it. Rates below 1 per 1,000 are shown to two decimals so that small differences are visible.
